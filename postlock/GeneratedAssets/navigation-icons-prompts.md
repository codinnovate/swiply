## Final prompt set

The exact shared prompt below was used once per subject, replacing `{subject}` with:

- Home: `a simple rounded house with one arched doorway`
- Analytics: `three ascending rounded bars with a small upward swoosh`
- Leaderboard: `a rounded trophy cup with two handles on a small pedestal`
- Settings: `a chunky rounded six-tooth gear with one circular center hole`

Use case: stylized-concept. Production navigation icon asset for POSTLOCK iOS. One {subject}. Cohesive premium 3D satin ceramic icon in lavender #BBAEFF, pale lilac highlights, soft subtle shadows within the object. Front view, bold extremely simple silhouette readable at 26 pixels. Centered square composition with 12 percent margin. Genuinely transparent alpha background. No text, no extra decorations, no floor, no outer shadow, no UI, no frame. Same restrained soft sculptural style as a lavender flame and origami paper plane in a midnight productivity app.
